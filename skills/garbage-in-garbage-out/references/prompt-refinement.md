# Prompt Refinement

When consuming artifacts, precede the title with:
`Upstream: <names> | Re-derived: <load-bearing claims and actual source checks>`
Include failed/unavailable re-derivations and limits. Omit when none were consumed.
Use plain Markdown. Combine short fields for simple requests; this template is
not a requirement for a lengthy document or separate Requirements artifact.

## Task understanding

Objective and intended outcome; audience; original constraints and exclusions;
available context; observable success. Attribute user-provided facts and identify
actual inspected support. Distinguish `inferred` conclusions, `assumed` premises,
and `unknown` facts. Name missing primary material without implying it was read.

Include assumptions and open questions: resurface every relied-on assumption,
its dependent conclusion and what would resolve it. For consequential ambiguity,
state competing interpretations, their approach/risk implications, affected clauses
and focused clarification. Under G8, keep dependent work partial pending the answer;
continue unaffected refinement. For low-risk reversible gaps, state a provisional
assumption and proceed. Missing facts alone do not require blocking questions.

## Professional-depth assessment

- **Professional standard:** task-specific improvement in reasoning, method or
  evidence, or a justified finding that the existing instruction suffices.
- **Primary material:** original sources needed; distinguish inspected sources
  and actual checks from proposed executor investigation.
- **Methods and tools:** concrete techniques and their purpose for this task,
  or why no additional method/tool is warranted.
- **Independent verification:** observable/reproducible checks or independent
  evidence; separate expected evidence from observed results. Shared-origin
  agreement, AI confidence and user expectations alone are not verification.

All four lenses require meaningful proportional treatment, even for short prompts.
For an already-clear prompt, use four short labeled clauses rather than omitting
lenses; name the source text as primary material and a concrete correctness check.

## Refined prompt

Write the complete improved instruction here, ready to copy into a fresh chat
without reconstructing the assessment. Include the original goal, constraints,
exclusions, relevant professional methods, provisional assumptions, consequential
unknowns where relevant, and evidence/verification expectations. Do not refer to
"the above" for essential context. Do not invent requirements or unrelated work.
If G8 remains unresolved, label this a partial draft, identify dependent clauses
and do not claim final reusability until clarified.

Stop after refinement. Do not execute this prompt or automatically invoke siblings.
Optional later handoffs are suggestions, not completed work or execution grants.
