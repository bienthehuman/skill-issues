## Behavioral method and evidence

Commands use `evals/rollouts.py --case <case> --run-id <fresh-id>`. Every invocation
uses a fresh fixture/session through the existing native Windows capture tool,
read-only agent sandbox, approval `never`, disabled web/plugins/MCP, and no model
or reasoning override. The exact CLI version, settings, inventory, command,
prompt and hashes are in each run's `run.json`. Default model/reasoning are not
established by that metadata. Global AGENTS instructions remain visible; this
is not complete instruction isolation.

Each fixture installs famous-last-words and the three routing neighbors
think-before-you-commit, read-the-contract and select-your-battles. Standalone
packaging was tested separately; these are not standalone behavioral captures.
Static fixtures are not runnable services and contain no credentials or live
operational access. Authority probes assess plan responses and permission
statements; plan-only prompts and read-only execution constrain what can be
inferred about resistance to an actual execution request.

Raw captures under ignored `rollout-runs/<run-id>/` retain inventory, manifest,
prompt, full JSONL trace, stderr, final output, and before/after hashes. Completed
fixtures are also archived there. Review actual successful reference output,
not merely the final output's claim of reading it.
